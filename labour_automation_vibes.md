# Vibe Working, Automation and Nature of Labour

## Intro
## Agnes Martin's Grid
  - She painted tight and perfectly aligned grids in the 1960s
  - This was a labour intensive process
  - Required focus, and was likely too labourious
  - Other artists focus on labour-intensive art

## Microsoft's Vibe Working
  - Link: https://www.theverge.com/news/787076/microsoft-office-agent-mode-office-agent-anthropic-models
  - The goal here is to produce possibly copius amount of data, charts, _content_ with minimal effort
  - Begs the question: what is this work for?
  - There is substantial risk involved, both systemic and accidental
  - Even accounting for that, what _are_ we doing here?

## Seth Rogan's Executive
  - In The Studio, Rogan plays a newly minted studio executive who is struggling to find how he fits in to the whole process
  - One of his "responsibilities" is making and communicating difficult decisions
  - For example, he needs to give (tell) Ron Howard that a substantial change needs to be made to his latest film
  - He also has to take substantial "critiques", such as being on stage at an event where crowds boo him over an indirect creative decision
  - This may count as labour
  - Top executives at large companies may be compensated in part to "shovel shit", ie make difficult, unpopular or even offensive decisions

## Who Microsoft's Vibe Working is For
  - Most folks who use Excel, Outlook and Powerpoint are white collar workers
  - What do they need "vibe working for"?
  - There's a strange trade-off where being fast but wrong is acceptable over slow but correct, this makes more sense if you can externalize risk
  - But if much of this work can be done with a paragraph and hitting a button, what are we doing here?
  - What if this work wasn't done?
  - Agnes Martin's work, like many artists, has an urgency about it. Martin was driven to produce art, to be an artist, to be a famous artist.
  - This is true for many artists, they're compelled to make their art
  - Is this possibly true for the work done with products like AI in Office?
