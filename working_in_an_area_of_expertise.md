In some cheerful news, I've currently been working at a new company as a Senior SDET. It's been a couple of months and things are going pretty well.

One notable thing about working at this company is that the products is a platform for mathematics educators for math activities to be used in the classroom. For me, this is an ideal company for me to work for.

When I was starting out in software quality assurance and testing, I remember reading an article providing tips for finding and being successful at software testing jobs. One of the pieces of advice in that article - now lost to the sands of time - was to find a position working with software in an area you personally find interesting. The intuition was straightforward: if you're interested in a topic or field, you'll be more invested to work with and learn about the software in that field. For example, if you like photography, a photography-based app or platform might be a good fit for a testing job. I remember thinking this advice was pretty good, even including the fact that (too?) often job searchers don't always get to apply or work at organizations or on products they personally interesting.

So, as a certified math nerd, working with a platform that is based on math questions and answers is pretty exciting. I've already been able to use some of my old math skills (writing LaTeX) for testing purposes in this new role, which I'm calling a win.

Let's see what else comes out of working with something you care about on a personal level.
